Three steps

How to submit via WhatsApp

  1. 1

    Message the bot

    Tap the WhatsApp button above or scan the QR code below. The chat opens with a ready-made first message.

  2. 2

    Answer a few questions

    Tell the bot what you saw, where, and when. A best guess at the species is fine, and a photo helps verification.

  3. 3

    We take it from there

    Our team reviews every report. Once verified, approved sightings appear on the public map.

Good reports

What to include

  • Species — your best guess is enough
  • Location — GPS coordinates or the nearest atoll/island
  • Date and time of the encounter
  • Group size — a rough range works
  • A photo, if you have one
