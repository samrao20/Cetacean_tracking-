Submit a sighting

Saw a dolphin or whale? One WhatsApp message is all it takes — our bot walks you through the rest.

Open the WhatsApp bot

Three steps

How to submit

  1. 1

    Message the bot

    Tap the WhatsApp button on this page (or scan the QR code below). The chat opens with a ready-made first message — just press send.

  2. 2

    Answer a few questions

    The bot asks what you saw, where, and when. Share whatever you have — a best guess at the species is fine, and a photo helps our team confirm it.

  3. 3

    We take it from there

    Our team reviews every report. Once verified, your sighting appears on the public map and in the dashboard statistics.

Good reports

What to include

  • Species — your best guess is enough; the species guide can help
  • Location — share your GPS position or name the nearest atoll or island
  • Date and time of the encounter
  • Group size — a rough range like "10–20" works
  • A photo, if you managed one — it makes verification much easier

Your privacy is protected: phone numbers are stored only as a one-way hash and never appear on the public site, and map locations are rounded to about 5 km. Read more on the About page.
