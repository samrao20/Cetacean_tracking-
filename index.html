The process

How it works

You spot, we verify, the science grows — all through a simple WhatsApp message.

  1. 1

    Spot a cetacean

    See a dolphin or whale? Open WhatsApp and send a message to our bot with a photo, your location, and what you saw.

    Via WhatsApp
  2. 2

    We verify

    Our team reviews each report — checking species identification, location, and photo. Only verified sightings appear on the public map.

    Expert reviewed
  3. 3

    Science grows

    Your sighting joins the map, the dashboard, and the long-term record — helping researchers understand cetacean distribution across the atolls.

    On the map instantly
